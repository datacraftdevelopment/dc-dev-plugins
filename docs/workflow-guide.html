The operating model

You set direction. AI does the detailed work.

Planning starts with your intended outcome and the decisions that matter to you. AI reads the project, tests assumptions and uses another model to resolve technical questions before bringing you a short recommendation.

01 · ShapeYou define success and priorities.
02 · ResolveAI researches and cross-reviews the details.
03 · AgreeYou approve scope and meaningful frontend choices.
04 · BuildAI implements and verifies within the agreement.
05 · AcceptEvidence proves the result; you judge reserved product choices.
Decisions without the interrogation

Bring you choices worth making.

AI handles

Repository investigation, technical tradeoffs already delegated, implementation, tests, ordinary fixes and scoped review rounds. A routine defect found by one reviewer needs independent confirmation before it is applied.

You handle

Product priorities, unresolved scope changes, important frontend direction and consequential commitments. A disagreement with your stated direction stays visible; technical consensus does not override it.

The accepted intent records the execution agreement. An installed plugin or a draft template does not authorize new work. Existing projects retain their current workflow until they adopt the agreement.

Cross-review stays central

Different models, explicit findings, visible decisions.

Ringer sends the same frozen artifact to Astra and Fable. The lead checks their evidence, records agreements and disagreements, applies confirmed routine fixes within scope, and gives you a digest.

A technical split gets a targeted investigation and a focused follow-up. The agreed review limit prevents endless rounds. Unresolved product choices return to you with a recommendation and the reason.

Multimodal evidence

A green code test cannot prove a good interface.

Each acceptance criterion names the evidence it needs. The builder, UI runner and fresh verifier have separate jobs. The verifier examines the actual captures and checks persisted state independently.

ChannelWhat it establishes
AutomatedExecutable checks for the specified behavior.
UIActual interactions and inspected captures of relevant screens, errors and recovery states.
StateThe expected data was saved correctly, checked independently of what the screen says.
HumanYour decision on reserved design or product criteria.

Required evidence may pass, fail or be blocked. Missing evidence cannot become “shipped.” Mutating UI scenarios are never blindly replayed.

Using it on a real project

One clear brief starts the work.

“Build the client intake flow. Show me the proposed screens before settling the frontend. Use AI to resolve technical details, run the agreed cross-reviews and tests, and continue within the accepted scope. Bring me unresolved product decisions and the final acceptance evidence.”

The agent reads the project, drafts the intended result and recommends the technical approach. Your acceptance records the scope, reserved frontend checkpoints and execution limits in the intent. Later sessions resume that agreement; a new project or a broader scope needs its own acceptance.

During the build, expect concise progress and a review digest. At delivery, inspect what changed, which checks passed and which evidence is still blocked. A blocked UI check stays visible even when the code tests pass.

Continuity

Keep What’s Next and Stepping Away.

What’s Next

Resume the work you named without asking you to choose it again. When there is no chosen task, propose one or two useful options. Separate work AI can do from decisions waiting for you.

Checkpoint

Re-aim a long session against its intent. Record drift and the next action without forcing a close-and-reopen ritual.

Stepping Away

Record what shipped, what remains and who owns active work. Local bookkeeping can proceed under existing authorization. A chat closing does not schedule future execution.

Bounded autonomy

Continue intelligently; stop honestly.

The runtime reserves a ticket’s attempt budget before dispatch. Restarting the loop or changing the candidate cannot erase spent attempts. One diagnosed recovery is allowed within the agreed total budget; unknown failures do not automatically become repair work.

A graceful pause acknowledges the request, drains already dispatched work and reconciles it before reporting “paused.” A killed or timed-out process remains an abort. Session summaries must use the observed runtime state.
